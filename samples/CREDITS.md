The Littlest Tokyo file is used unchanged (Draco compressed, as shipped with three.js r170). Türk Bakkalı was compressed for the web with [glTF Transform](https://gltf-transform.dev/) (Draco meshes, WebP textures; 47.7 MB → 2.4 MB); the geometry and textures are otherwise unchanged. The card and thumbnail images of both models are renders made in Deep Window.
